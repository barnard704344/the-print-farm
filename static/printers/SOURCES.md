## P1S and AMS Combined Cutout

`bambu-p1s-ams.png` is a generated transparent cutout based on the combined product
photo from [Bambu Lab’s P1S product page](https://bambu-lab-uk.myshopify.com/products/p1s)
([original image](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0573/5320/7868/files/Group48168.png?v=1708411583)).
It was prepared with the built-in imagegen tool so the printer and roof-mounted
AMS share one perspective and scale. The manufacturer’s product imagery and
trademarks remain attributable to Bambu Lab; this file is separate from the
OrcaSlicer images listed above.

The complete generation prompt and input details are saved in
[bambu-p1s-ams.prompt.txt](bambu-p1s-ams.prompt.txt).
